## Brand & Style

This design system defines a high-precision aerospace intelligence environment designed for global travelers, dispatch operations, and flight command personnel. The interface bridges the density of professional financial terminals with the tactile polish of luxury consumer hardware interfaces.

### Core Tenets
- **Cinematic Precision**: Information architecture mirrors high-altitude heads-up displays (HUD), where telemetry, vector routes, and predictive models maintain visual composure under dense cognitive loads.
- **Controlled Urgency**: Disruption and delay states do not scream; they articulate calculated operational realities through surgical color cues and calibrated glow parameters.
- **Tactile Obsidian Depth**: Surfaces feel milled from synthetic obsidian glass, relying on optical transparency, micro-borders, and high-frequency data grids instead of opaque flat planes.
- **Editorial Legibility**: Human prose is warm and structural, contrasting sharply against rigid, tabular monospaced flight matrices.

## Colors

The color system operates on an ultra-dark astronomical scale. Pigments are calibrated strictly for high dynamic contrast against deep spatial backgrounds.

### Color Distribution & Application
- **Primary Accent (`#FF5E1E`)**: Reserved exclusively for active tracking needles, critical live CTAs, system alerts, route trajectory origins, and active state indicators. Never used for non-interactive ambient fills.
- **Telemetry Cyan (`#38BDF8`)**: Designated for navigational vectors, radar sweeps, Mach airspeed indicators, and AI predictive suggestions.
- **Flight Operational States**:
  - `Emerald (#10B981)`: Nominal schedule, stable airspace, confirmed gate departure.
  - `Amber (#F59E0B)`: Minor deviation, taxi delay under 25m, runway congestion advisory.
  - `Crimson (#EF4444)`: Diverted, grounded, gate conflict, or severe disruption alert.
- **Obsidian Neutrals**:
  - Deep Canvas: `#08090C`
  - Elevated Container / Base Layer: `#0B0E14`
  - Card & HUD Surface: `#10141D`
- **Text & Foreground Hierarchy**:
  - Primary Telemetry & Headers: `#F7F8FA`
  - Sub-labels & Column Headers: `#94A3B8`
  - Muted Vectors, Timestamps & Dividers: `#64748B`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates structural communication from real-time flight vectors:
- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an aerodynamic, wide-aperture structure with technical authority. Used for platform positioning, section headers, and prompt titles.
- **System Prose (`Inter`)**: Neutral, highly legible rendering engine for flight recommendations, disruption explanations, and customer advisory copy.
- **Flight Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for all IATA/ICAO codes (`SGA-882`, `HND`, `JFK`), altitude (`FL380`), velocity (`Mach 0.84`), UTC timestamps, coordinates, and gate assignments. Always rendered with tabular figures (`tnum`).
- **Data Labels (`label-caps`)**: Always rendered in uppercase with expanded tracking for immediate identification in peripheral vision.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture balances large geographic maps and flight corridors with precision HUD modules.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>=1440px)**: 12-column dynamic command grid. Outer margins set at `margin` (`3rem`), with `gutter` (`1.5rem`). Layout accommodates a fixed 320px telemetry inspection rail and a fluid primary airspace view.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px)**: 8-column layout. Flight route view remains sticky at top-level split screen; telemetry collapses to modular swipe cards below.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout. Outer canvas margins contract to `margin-mobile` (`1rem`) and gutters tighten to `gutter-mobile` (`0.75rem`). Overlays transform into floating bottom sheets with gesture dismissals.

### Sub-Pixel Spatial Rhythm
- Layout frames are bound by a continuous micro-grid pattern (32px intervals rendered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`), simulating avionics glass displays.
- Visual elements adhere strictly to multiples of 4px. Card interiors utilize `space-md` (`1rem`) padding on mobile and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through photonic transparency, edge luminescence, and optical refraction rather than diffuse grey shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Absolute background (`#08090C`) with ambient radar coordinate lines and passive sub-pixel grid intersections.
- **Level 1 (Docked Structural Rail & Nav)**: `rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.75)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a bottom or right hairline boundary of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Telemetry Modules & HUD Cards)**: `rgba(16, 20, 29, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Outer outline: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Inner top hairline: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` to simulate top-down ambient light incidence.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts & Disruptive Intelligence Overlays)**: `rgba(18, 23, 34, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`. Outlines shift from neutral white to tinted warnings during disruption:
  - Critical Disruption: Border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)` accompanied by an ambient edge glow: `0 0 32px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`.
  - Nominal Focus: Border `1px solid rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.35)` with orange radial glow: `0 0 24px rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.10)`.

## Shapes

The design language favors low-radius technical beveling (`roundedness: 1`). Soft curves are constrained to maintain the feel of aerospace machinery and glass instruments:
- Base components (buttons, input fields, tags, telemetry pills): `0.25rem` (4px).
- Flight cards, HUD modules, command bars: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modals, large operational sheets: `0.75rem` (12px).
- System status nodes, radar sweeps, and live beacons: Full circular rendering (`50%`).

## Components

### 1. Navigation & Command Bar
- **Wing/Radar Emblem**: Positioned top-left with a pulsing green telemetry node (`#10B981`) indicating active WebSocket telemetry streaming.
- **System Navigation**: Horizontal layout utilizing `Space Grotesk` uppercase text. Active links display an underline micro-bar in `#FF5E1E` with a subtle vertical bloom.
- **AI Command Prompt ("Your journey. Under control.")**:
  - Centerpiece search & NLP bar built inside an elevated glass pill-container (`rgba(16, 20, 29, 0.8)`).
  - Search trigger styled with high-contrast JetBrains Mono placeholder text ("Search a flight number or ask Songa AI...").
  - Quick action tags integrated into the input trailing edge: `[Track Flight]` and `[Watch Flight]`, styled as micro-buttons with keyboard shortcut tooltips (`⌘K`).

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#FF5E1E`, label `#FFFFFF` in `Space Grotesk` Medium. Hover state transitions to `#FF7A3D` with an active glow `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / HUD Button**: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F7F8FA`. Hover initiates subtle cyan highlight (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)` border and text).
- **Destructive / Crisis Alert Action**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `1px solid #EF4444`, text `#EF4444`.

### 3. Floating Telemetry Cards & HUDs
- **Telemetry Readout Matrix**:
  - Grid layouts containing origin/destination pairs (`JFK ➔ HND`) separated by aircraft vector vectors.
  - Telemetry variables (`FL380`, `Mach 0.84`, `GS 540kts`, `Wind 270°/42kt`) placed in isolated micro-boxes with muted uppercase labels directly above bright monospace values.
- **Predictive Engine Gauge**:
  - Radial or linear probability meter displaying on-time predictive probability index.
  - Dynamic route glow behind the card reflecting status (`#10B981` nominal, `#F59E0B` cautionary, `#EF4444` rerouting in progress).

### 4. Chips & Status Badges
- Strict monospaced text rendering (`telemetry-sm`).
- Height locked at 22px, padding `0 8px`.
- Composed of an edge stroke and a tinted interior:
  - Live On-Time: Border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, fill `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`, text `#10B981`. Includes a pulsing 4px dot indicator.
  - Active Reroute: Border `rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.3)`, fill `rgba(255, 94, 30, 0.08)`, text `#FF5E1E`.

### 5. Input Fields
- Dark recessed background (`#0B0E14`) with a sharp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Focus state creates an instantaneous transition to `#FF5E1E` border with an interior inset shadow highlight.
- Monospace support for flight input fields with auto-capitalization and automatic sector formatting (`AAA-####`).

### 6. Lists & Flight Data Tables
- Row structures built with alternating sub-pixel tinting or isolated by horizontal hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`).
- Hovering over a row highlights the corresponding vector line on the background radar map.
- Columns utilize strict tabular alignments: text left-aligned, flight codes and statuses center-aligned, numeric telemetry right-aligned.
